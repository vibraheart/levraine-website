Two layers

The weave breathes. The beeswax keeps.

The outer bag is a breathable linen blend, printed with the loaf that started it all. Inside sits a cotton liner coated with pure beeswax, which helps hold in the moisture that keeps a crumb soft.

The liner lifts out, so you can shake away the crumbs and wash each layer its own way.

How it works

Three small gestures.

  1. i.

    Tuck the loaf inside

    Once it has cooled, set the bread into the liner.

  2. ii.

    Press out the air

    A gentle press along the bag, from the bottom up.

  3. iii.

    Roll and clip

    Roll the top down and fasten the strap to keep the freshness in.

Care

Lift the liner out and shake away the crumbs. Rinse the liner in cold water, keep it away from heat, and let it dry in the open air. How to store sourdough bread →

Questions

Before you choose.

What size are LEVRAINE bread bags?

Each bag measures 42 × 33 cm (16.5 × 13 in) laid flat, before rolling. That fits a round sourdough loaf or a pair of baguettes. Each set has two bags.

What are the bags made of?

The outer bag is a linen blend. The liner is 100% cotton coated in 100% beeswax, and it lifts out.

Can I keep bread in the freezer in it?

Yes. The bags are made for the counter or the freezer, so one bag can hold today’s loaf while the other waits in the freezer.

How do I clean a beeswax bread bag?

Lift the liner out and shake out the crumbs. Rinse the liner in cold water, keep it away from heat, and let it air dry.

Where can I buy LEVRAINE bread bags?
